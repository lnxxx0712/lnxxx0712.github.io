About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Language Science and Technology (LST) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, supervised by Prof. Shaonan Wang in the LINC Lab (Language, Intelligence, Neuroscience & Cognition).

My research focuses on the neural and computational mechanisms of language understanding, particularly how linguistic information is represented and organized in the human brain, and how computational models can help explain these processes. I am also interested in memory and its close interplay with language.
